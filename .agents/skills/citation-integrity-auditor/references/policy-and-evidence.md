# Policy and evidence basis

- [ACL 2026 statement on hallucinated references](https://2026.aclweb.org/acl_statement/): authors
  must manually confirm every cited source; affected accepted papers returned to normal resubmission.
- [HalluCitation Matters, ACL 2026](https://aclanthology.org/2026.acl-long.2189/): database/fuzzy
  candidate generation followed by original-PDF and web-based manual verification.
- [Phantom References](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.00738): multi-index resolution with unresolved
  cases escalated to web re-verification; RefChecker released as a secondary tool.
- [ACL publication ethics](https://www.aclweb.org/adminwiki/index.php/ACL_Policy_on_Publication_Ethics):
  authors remain responsible for accurate, transparent use of generative assistance.

These sources motivate the workflow. Do not cite them in an unrelated manuscript merely because
the audit used their procedures.
